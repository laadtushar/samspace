## The three parts, and what each is for

**Metrics, hooks and format go in italics.** They are context, not the deliverable. Nobody records
from them.

**The script is the focus.** Transcribed from the audio, literal, with timestamps. Never
paraphrased, never taken from the caption.

**The formula is what makes it reusable.** Strip the specifics, leave the skeleton with blanks.
This is the part that lets one outlier produce ten pieces.

**"My version" always exists.** Even for a rejected card — knowing why a mold does not fit is
worth as much as knowing why one does.

**The caption is a separate job.** No good outlier repeats the video in its caption. Some carry
only the CTA, some the fine print, some the series counters, some the actual advert with address
and phone. Copy the *shape*, not the words.

---

## Two checks before the card is finished

```
[ ] First transcript line reads [ 0.0s]
[ ] Sentences in "my version" <= steps in the formula
```

The first catches a truncated transcript, which puts the wrong hook on the card.
The second catches padding, which buries the template.
