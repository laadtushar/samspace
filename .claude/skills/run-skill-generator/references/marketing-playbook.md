# Marketing playbook (researched Oct 2026)

Treat numbers from vendors and agencies as directional; prefer what the
account's own Insights show.

## Hooks
- The first ~3 seconds decide most drop-off; lead with the payoff or a feeling,
  not set-up (Quso, Nereo). Check the first 3 s with the sound off.
- Lead with a behaviour or feeling the viewer recognises, not a clinical term;
  reveal the term mid-video (house rule from `samvriti-reel-scripts`).
- Write 4–5 hook options; if the account has **Trial reels**, test two hooks on
  the same cut and keep the winner.

## Length
- Guidance conflicts: 7–15 s cuts tend to complete most; 30–60 s cuts tend to
  earn more saves and shares (Nereo, Quso). So ship both: a ~10 s teaser, two
  ~30 s cuts, and the full piece.

## Editing for retention
- Remove pauses over ~0.4 s inside sentences; it can shorten a talking head
  noticeably without changing a word (Async). Measure silences on the audio.
- Change the framing at each new idea — a punch-in at cuts and section changes
  (Async).
- Pattern interrupts every 6–8 s on long talking heads: zoom, type moment, cut
  (agency practice; directional).
- Captions: clean, restrained word-by-word type is replacing loud bouncing
  captions ("dynamic minimalism", Joyspace). Most viewers start with sound off,
  so the video must read without audio.

## Captions and hashtags
- Put the topic words in the first line; keep the caption short; end with a
  soft CTA in the account's voice.
- 3–8 specific hashtags beat 20+ generic ones (Quso).
- Add a question for comments and a pinned comment that invites one-word replies.

## Repurposing (one video → a week of posts)
Reel A (best 30 s) → carousel of the key lines → Reel B (the reframe) →
type-only cut for LinkedIn/WhatsApp → quote post → full piece + YouTube Shorts.
Stories: poll → reel share → link sticker.
